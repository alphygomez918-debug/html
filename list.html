Planet Earth - Quick guide

Our planet is full of incredible recors, species and facts. Here is a quick quide using a list!

Steps to protecting our planet

  1. Switch of lights when you leave a room
  2. Use a reusable water bottle every day
  3. Walk or cycle instead of travelling by car
  4. Plant a tree in your neighbourhood
  5. Spread awareness - talk to your friend

Incredible natural wonders:

  • Amazon rainforest - home to 10% of all the species on Earth
  • The Great Barrier Reef in Australia
  • Mount Everest in Nepal
  • Victoria Falls in Zambia and Zimbabwe
  • Amazon Rainforest in South America
