💪 Why your body needs Exercise

Your body is lika a supercar — the more you use it the stronger it gets! Kids who move for 60 minutes every day are happier, sleep better and do better at school.

Dancing, swimming, football, yoga — anything that gets your heart beating faster counts. Keep moving and have fun

☀ Quick Health Tips

  • 🍎 Eat five fruits and veggies every day
  • 💪 Drink eight glasses of water daily
  • 🌟 Sleep 9-11
  • 👤 Wash your hands frequently
